🧭 Project Compass · Internal

Your Portfolio's Future, Simulated

Compass is a tool that shows investors what could happen to their money over the next 5 years — the good case, the bad case, and the most likely case — using math that banks already use, but presented simply.

What it is

📊 A "Portfolio GPS"

Instead of just showing past performance, Compass looks forward. It runs thousands of "what-if" scenarios on your real stock holdings and tells you: how likely you are to make money, how much you could gain or lose, and what your biggest risks are — all in plain language.

🤖 AI-Powered Health Check

Compass also gives your portfolio a health score out of 100, graded A through F. It checks whether your investments are spread out enough, whether your stocks are trending up or down, and whether the risk is worth the reward. Then an AI writes a short summary with one clear action to take.

How it works
1

You pick a portfolio

Choose from pre-built investor profiles (e.g. "Growth", "Dividend", "Tech") or connect your own holdings.

2

Compass runs 1,000+ simulations

It uses 5 years of real market data to generate thousands of possible futures — including market crashes and bull runs.

3

You see three outcomes

Bear Case (things go badly), Base Case (most likely), and Bull Case (things go well) — with projected values in INR and growth rates.

4

AI gives you plain-English advice

A headline, your top risk, your best opportunity, and one actionable recommendation — no jargon, no charts to decode.

Why it matters

💡 No one else does this for retail investors in India

Big banks and hedge funds run these simulations every day. Retail platforms like Groww and Zerodha don't offer anything close. Compass brings that institutional-grade analysis to everyday investors — in a format they can actually understand.
